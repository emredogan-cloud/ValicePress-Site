| Finding | Evidence | Action |
|---|---|---|

| "Valice Classics #15" was used by two books (`puzzles-old-and-new` carried a volume number nobody had verified) | `catalog-identity.test.ts` | The unverified number was **removed**, not invented |
| The catalogue was never tested against the world, only against itself | — | `catalog-identity.test.ts` (13 tests): slugs, ASINs and ISBNs unique across the catalogue; a series number used once; every Amazon URL is the one its ASIN produces; no ASIN belongs to two books; a KDP-Select ebook is never offered here; **every ASIN has a row of evidence read from the live page — title, format, ISBN — that agrees with the registry** |
| The verifier was reading Amazon's robot-check stub | `fetch` got a 2 KB page for pages `curl` read in full | `verify-amazon-asins.mjs` now fetches with `curl`; 55 / 55 read |
| `/ebooks` listed only ebooks sold here, which hid the Kindle-only romances | | `/ebooks` lists every book a reader can obtain as an ebook, here or on Kindle (34 of 36) |
| The quick view's "Buy on Amazon" could lead to another edition's page | | It leads to the **selected** edition's own URL; a test fails if book A's title sits beside book B's link |

Rules the catalogue enforces (all in tests): publication is data (`websiteStatus: "published"` beside its blockers); **no ASIN without a live KDP edition, no price that cannot be pointed at, no rating without a review, no cover that has not been seen**; a Select-enrolled book's ebook is never sold here (test untouched); `price_cents = 0` means *not sold here* — shown as such, with no JSON-LD `Offer` and sorted after every priced book.

## 5. Ridge Runner — a decision, not a defect

The brief lists *Ridge Runner* among the priority books. It has **no live KDP edition**, and the project rule is "no ASIN without a live edition", so its record is a `draft`: images, previews and quotations are staged, and `websiteStatus: "published"` plus its ASINs are the only things missing. It is pinned second so that publishing it is a catalogue edit and nothing else. Until then it is absent from every listing, the sitemap and `/books/ridge-runner` (404) — the matrix (`FINAL-BOOK-MATRIX.md`) shows it as **HELD**, not as a pass.

## 6. Not verified by this audit

- Prices are the ones read off the live pages on 2026-10-07 (the catalogue's `priceBasis` field says where each came from); Amazon prices move.
- A page count for an ebook is the built interior's, not Amazon's estimate (Weather Permitting: 292 built, 306 on Amazon).
- Nothing was loaded into the production database. **The World Games ebook's file must be replaced first.** The master in R2 under `books/the-great-book-of-world-games/master/v1/master.pdf` is the **182-page** revision built 2026-09-19 — its own front matter says "fifty-six games … thirty-nine cultures" under a title page that says 63 and 45 — while the catalogue now describes the **258-page** edition. The revised file was rebuilt from the current print interior on 2026-10-07 (`node scripts/catalog/build-digital-editions.mjs the-great-book-of-world-games`) and **has not been uploaded**; `scripts/catalog/upload-masters.mjs` (dry run by default, `--commit` to write, an object of the same size is never overwritten) puts it in the bucket. **Upload it before the catalogue is loaded into production, or buyers receive the earlier revision under the new description.** Neither step is authorised or done — see `FINAL-RELEASE-REPORT-2026.md` §10.
- The catalogue's per-book `blockers` field holds 184 operational notes across the 37 books (where a price was read, what KDP still needs, what is the Founder's). They are the catalogue's own record and are not reproduced here; the ones that touch the site are in `PHASE-LOG.md` and `FINAL-RELEASE-REPORT-2026.md`.
