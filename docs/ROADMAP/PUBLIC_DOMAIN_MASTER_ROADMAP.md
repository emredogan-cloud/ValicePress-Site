## 1. How candidates became slots

The research catalog holds 144 candidates. They are not 144 production slots, and the
gap is the most important thing on this page.

### Eligibility is a gate, not a score

A candidate may occupy a production slot only if **every** one of these is true:

| Test | Rule |
|---|---|
| Tier | Not REJECTED, not HOLD |
| Rights | GREEN on every layer the edition would use |
| Cultural gate | None open |
| Source | A verified, usable identifier exists (PG or IA) |
| Not already ours | Not a book Valice already sells |
| Not a trap | Not one of the three reprint/never-use reference entries |

Applying that gate to the catalog:

| | Count |
|---|---:|
| Candidates in the research catalog | 144 |
| **Eligible for a production slot** | **97** |
| Not schedulable | 47 |
| — HOLD (missing death year, blocked date, cultural gate, unusable source) | 34 |
| — REJECTED (RED on a layer, or a reprint trap) | 5 |
| — YELLOW rights not resolved | 6 |
| — Already a live Valice product | 2 |

**50 of the 97 eligible candidates are assigned to Phases 1–10.**
The remaining 47 are eligible and unscheduled — they are listed in §5 as
the Phase 11+ reserve rather than padded into invented phases.

### Ordering is Factory ROI, corrected by judgement

**Factory ROI = expected commercial value ÷ estimated production effort.**

Expected value is a weighted blend of commercial potential (30%), Valice catalog fit (28%),
differentiation (22%), direct ebook potential (12%) and bundle/series potential (8%).
Effort is the catalog's production-practicality axis, where a higher number means easier.

The ratio was computed for every eligible candidate — and then **not followed mechanically**,
because it inverts. Dividing by a low practicality score inflates ROI, so the raw ranking puts
*A History of Chess* (900 pages, practicality 18) and *The Grammar of Ornament* (112 colour
plates, practicality 20) at the top. Those are real opportunities and terrible first books.

The correction that actually drives the sequence is **source type**:

| Source | Count | What it costs |
|---|---:|---|
| Project Gutenberg | 87 | Proof-read HTML. The Dudeney build measured parsing at ~25 minutes. |
| Internet Archive scan | 10 | OCR, normalisation, and usually diagram redrawing. A pipeline that does not yet exist. |

So Phase 1 is entirely Gutenberg-sourced, and every Internet Archive title is held until
**Phase 2**, where the scan pipeline is built once and amortised across five titles that
all need it. This is the single largest scheduling decision in the roadmap.

The apparatus is the entire product, and it must be real. Werner carries the opposite risk — plate artist unattributed, so original art or a text-only design is mandatory.

### Expected outputs

Five complete editions: EPUB + print-ready PDF + typographic covers + Paddle products + website product pages + companions + KDP upload packages. One three-book bundle (The Stoic Library) with a live anchor.
